Costa Mesa electrical questions

Useful answers before the first call.

Can I request work for a retail or tenant space?

Yes. Describe the existing space, planned use, equipment needs, desired timing, and any landlord or building-management requirements.

Do you work on panels and dedicated circuits?

Yes. Panel, subpanel, breaker, capacity, and dedicated-circuit work can be reviewed. Photos of the panel and labels are useful when safe to take.

Is the website form appropriate for an emergency?

No. For smoke, fire, arcing, or immediate danger, leave the area and call 911. For urgent but safe-to-approach concerns, call the business directly.
