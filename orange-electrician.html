Common questions

Before requesting electrical service in Orange.

What kinds of properties do you support?

Professionals Electric provides residential, commercial, and industrial electrical services. The form asks for the property type so the request can be reviewed in context.

What photos should I include?

When safe, include the electrical panel, label, affected device, equipment nameplate, and a wider view showing access. Never approach exposed or energized parts for a photo.

What if there is smoke, fire, or immediate danger?

Leave the unsafe area and call 911 for fire, smoke, arcing, or immediate danger. Do not rely on a website form for an emergency.
